# TempMailGrab

> TempMailGrab is a privacy-first temporary email service with private 24-hour inboxes, real-time WebSocket delivery, automatic OTP and verification-link extraction, attachments, and a developer REST API.

## Primary pages

- [Temporary email inbox](https://tempmailgrab.com/): Generate and use a private temporary inbox.
- [Developer API](https://tempmailgrab.com/api-docs): Authentication, endpoints, webhooks, custom domains, examples, and OpenAPI.
- [OpenAPI 3.1 document](https://tempmailgrab.com/api/openapi.json): Machine-readable API contract.
- [JavaScript SDK](https://www.npmjs.com/package/tempmailgrab): Typed zero-dependency API client, Playwright fixtures, and webhook verification. [Source](https://github.com/sathishbanoth-coder/tempmailgrab-js).
- [Email testing API](https://tempmailgrab.com/email-testing-api): Playwright, Cypress, and CI email testing workflows.
- [Temporary email guide](https://tempmailgrab.com/guides): When temporary email is appropriate and when it is not.
- [Resource center](https://tempmailgrab.com/resources): Curated entry points for temporary email, privacy, developer testing, and compatibility research.
- [Compatibility research](https://tempmailgrab.com/research/disposable-email-compatibility): Tested platform compatibility, methodology, and reuse terms.
- [Compatibility dataset](https://tempmailgrab.com/research/disposable-email-compatibility.json): CC BY 4.0 machine-readable data.
- [Compatibility dataset CSV](https://tempmailgrab.com/research/disposable-email-compatibility.csv): Tabular CC BY 4.0 distribution.
- [Security](https://tempmailgrab.com/security): Architecture, retention, HTML sanitization, and responsible disclosure.
- [Privacy](https://tempmailgrab.com/privacy): Data collection, retention, and deletion policy.
- [About](https://tempmailgrab.com/about): Product purpose, architecture, and editorial responsibility.
